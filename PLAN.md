## The Three Open Problems

The BH propulsion concept has been stalled for 15 years by three unsolved engineering problems. No published paper solves any of them. Our paper solves all three.

### Problem 1: Formation — How do you create a micro black hole?

**Prior work:**
- Crane & Westmoreland (2009): proposed gamma-ray laser implosion. No detailed mechanism.
- Alvarez-Dominguez et al. (2024, PRL): proved gamma-ray kugelblitze are impossible — Schwinger pair production dissipates the photon concentration before gravitational collapse.
- Page (2025): rebutted, arguing idealized processes could work, but concedes practical impossibility.
- Lee, Nair & Weinberg (1992): showed monopoles transition to BHs at critical coupling (theoretical, not engineering).

**Our solution:** Gravitational collapse of accumulated BPS magnetic monopoles. Novel. No published paper proposes this as an engineering method.

### Problem 2: Reflection — How do you reflect GeV Hawking radiation?

**Prior work:**
- Crane & Westmoreland (2009): asserted an "electron gas mirror" with no material, no physics, no calculation.
- Lee (2015, JBIS): abandoned reflection entirely. Used titanium absorption at 46.5% capture efficiency. Result: max velocity 0.0001c. Proved the drive is useless without a real mirror.
- Community consensus: "There is no known physics for such a reflector" (Centauri Dreams, iSETI, Wikipedia).

**Our solution:** CFL/CCSC strange quark matter mirrors reflecting via three mechanisms:
1. Superconducting gap (photons below ~50 MeV): R = 1 - 10^-30
2. Bethe-Bloch ionization stopping (charged particles at GeV): ~80 MeV/fm at nuclear density, 8 GeV stopping in 100 fm
3. Strong-force elastic scattering (hadrons): reflection at nuclear-density surface

**This is the most important novel contribution.** It answers Lee's 2015 paper directly and reopens the entire BH drive concept.

### Problem 3: Confinement — How do you keep the BH at the reflector's focus?

**Prior work:**
- Crane & Westmoreland (2009): suggested "particle beams" to push the BH. No mechanism, no calculation, no stability analysis.
- No other published proposal exists.

**Our solution:** Magnetically charged BH (via monopole feeding) confined by Meissner repulsion from superconducting SQM secondary mirror. Inherently stable (1/r^2 restoring force). Novel.

### B. Physics to Verify or Derive More Rigorously

1. **Hawking spectrum species counting at kT = 2.68 GeV**
   - Reproduce f = 2.85 x 10^-3 from Page's greybody factors species by species
   - Show partial suppression of charm, tau, bottom explicitly
   - Confirm the 91% non-neutrino fraction
   - Cross-check against BlackHawk numerical code (Arbey & Auffinger 2019) if possible

2. **SQM mirror reflectivity (NEW — the key derivation)**
   - Derive CFL gap reflectivity from optical conductivity of CFL quark matter
   - Calculate Bethe-Bloch stopping power at nuclear density (4 x 10^17 kg/m^3) for GeV protons, pions, muons
   - Verify 100 fm thickness provides sufficient stopping (~8 GeV)
   - Calculate strong-interaction mean free path at nuclear density for hadrons
   - Show sensitivity: does the drive work at R = 1 - 10^-10 instead of 1 - 10^-30?
   - Cite Alford et al. on CFL electromagnetic properties for grounding

3. **Meissner confinement equilibrium**
   - Derive the force on a monopole charge inside a superconducting hemisphere
   - Compute linearized restoring force for axial and lateral displacements
   - Derive oscillation frequency for small perturbations
   - Show the equilibrium is dynamically stable

4. **Oppenheimer-Snyder collapse of the monopole lobes**
   - Verify free-fall time for two-lobe geometry
   - Confirm horizon formation before lobe merger
   - Address BPS third-law issue (sub-extremal formation avoids it)

5. **BH mass optimization**
   - Derive optimal mass from competing constraints
   - Present as trade-space plot, not single design point

6. **Magnetic charge stability**
   - Compute Schwinger discharge timescale for electric charge quantitatively
   - Confirm magnetic charge stability (Schwinger monopole pair production negligible)
   - Derive charge adjustment schedule

7. **Reissner-Nordstrom corrections**
   - Confirm Schwarzschild approximation valid at 0.027% extremal
   - Cross-check Maldacena's Landau-level enhancement is negligible at this charge

### C. Open Questions to Investigate

### 2. Black Hole Mass Selection (~1,200 words)
- Competing constraints define a mass window
- Lifetime constraint: tau > mission duration
- Thrust constraint: F > minimum for vehicle mass class
- Temperature constraint: kT determines spectrum composition

### 3. Hawking Spectrum and Thrust (~1,200 words)
- Species counting at kT = 2.68 GeV using Page's greybody factors
- Emission factor f = 2.85 x 10^-3 derived species by species
- Spectrum composition: quarks/gluons 75%, neutrinos 9%, leptons 12%, photons 1%, other 3%
- Non-neutrino fraction: 91% of power is capturable (IF you have a mirror)
- Thrust = P_beam / c = 9.6 MN, Isp = 27.9 x 10^6 s (91% of photon rocket limit)
- **Comparison to Lee (2015):** Lee found 45.5% capture with titanium, yielding 0.0001c. With full 91% capture via SQM, performance increases by >1000x.
- **Figure 2:** Hawking spectrum composition bar chart
- **Table:** Performance comparison (this work vs. Crane & Westmoreland vs. Lee)

### 4. The Mirror: Strange Quark Matter Reflectors (~1,800 words)
- **The mirror problem:** no conventional material reflects GeV radiation. Lee (2015) proved this is the bottleneck.
- SQM in the CFL/CCSC phase as a broadband Hawking radiation reflector
- **Three reflection mechanisms:**
  1. Superconducting gap: all photons below ~50 MeV reflected by CFL gap. Derive R from optical conductivity.
  2. Ionization stopping: charged particles stopped by Bethe-Bloch at nuclear density. Calculate stopping power, show 100 fm sufficient for ~8 GeV.
  3. Strong-force scattering: hadrons reflected by nuclear-density surface within first fm.
- Only neutrinos escape (9% of emission)
- Primary mirror: f/0.25 SQM paraboloid (3,830 tonnes, 100 fm thick)
- Secondary mirror: SQM hemisphere (0.251 kg) behind BH for backward hemisphere capture
- Combined: full 4-pi solid angle capture minus neutrinos
- Sensitivity analysis: drive performance vs. reflectivity
- **Figure 3:** Mirror geometry cross-section diagram

### 5. Confinement: Magnetically Charged Black Holes (~1,500 words)
- **The confinement problem:** how to hold a BH at the mirror's focal point. No prior solution exists.
- Why electric charge fails: Schwinger discharge in nanoseconds (derive timescale)
- Why magnetic charge works: no light magnetic monopoles for Schwinger pair production
- BH carries magnetic charge g = 27,730 A m via monopole feeding
- Meissner repulsion from superconducting SQM secondary mirror
- **Equilibrium derivation:** F_Meissner = mu_0 g^2 / (32 pi r^2) = M_BH x a_ship at r = 1 mm
- **Stability analysis:** axial (1/r^2 provides restoring force), lateral (hemisphere geometry)
- Charge evolution over operational life (27,730 to 73,200 A m)
- **Figure 4:** Meissner force vs. distance with equilibrium point
- **Figure 5:** Confinement geometry detail (BH, secondary, field lines, equilibrium)

### 6. Formation: Monopole Gravitational Collapse (~1,500 words)
- BPS monopole properties: exact force cancellation between same-sign monopoles
- Only gravity remains — pressureless dust collapse
- Two-lobe dumbbell geometry (monopoles + antimonopoles segregated)
- Oppenheimer-Snyder collapse: derive free-fall time (0.47 s per lobe)
- Horizon formation before lobe merger (0.47 s vs. 0.92 s mutual free-fall)
- Merged BH inherits net magnetic charge
- Seed BH properties: 5,500 tonnes, kT = 1,920 GeV, 69 s unfed lifetime
- Mass growth via pellet injection (brief)
- **Table:** Formation parameters and seed BH properties

### 7. Discussion (~1,000 words)
- **Assumption sensitivity:** what breaks if each assumption fails (table from source doc)
- **Comparison to all prior work:** Crane & Westmoreland (formation), Lee (reflection), no prior (confinement)
- **The SQM mirror as the enabling technology:** without it, BH drives produce 0.0001c (Lee). With it, 0.12-0.64c.
- **Implications:** the mirror problem was the real bottleneck, not formation
- **Limitations:** five unverified assumptions, breeding cross-section uncalculated, CFL reflectivity needs more rigorous derivation
- **Future work:** full breeding cross-section calculation, lattice QCD constraints on CFL reflectivity, experimental SQM searches

### 8. Conclusion (~400 words)
- Summary: three solutions to three open problems
- The SQM mirror is the key contribution — it transforms BH propulsion from 0.0001c (Lee) to 0.12-0.64c
- Conditional feasibility under five stated assumptions
- The concept is now self-consistent; the remaining question is whether the assumptions hold
