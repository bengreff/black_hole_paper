# Reading List

Prioritized by importance to the paper. Read in this order.

## Tier 1: Read Before Writing (these shape the paper's framing)

- [ ] **Lee 2015** — "Acceleration of a Schwarzschild Kugelblitz Starship" (JBIS 68, 105)
  - **THE paper our SQM mirror answers.** Lee proved that with conventional materials (titanium), you get 46.5% energy capture and max velocity 0.0001c. He abandoned reflection and used absorption. Our SQM mirror gets 91% capture and 0.12-0.64c.
  - Understand exactly what he calculated, what he assumed, and where he gave up.
  - PDF available on ResearchGate.

- [ ] **Crane & Westmoreland 2009** — "Are Black Hole Starships Possible?" (arXiv:0908.1803)
  - The foundational paper we're extending. They proposed the concept but hand-waved the mirror ("electron gas") and confinement ("particle beams"). We solve both.
  - Note what they assume vs. what they derive.

- [ ] **Alvarez-Dominguez et al. 2024** — "No Black Holes from Light" (PRL 133, 041401; arXiv:2405.02389)
  - Kills gamma-ray kugelblitze. Our paper's motivation depends on this result.
  - Understand the Schwinger mechanism argument precisely.

- [ ] **Maldacena 2020** — "Comments on Magnetic Black Holes" (arXiv:2004.06084)
  - Must confirm our operating charge (0.027% extremal) is below the Landau-level enhancement threshold.
  - Critical for our confinement section: we use magnetically charged BHs.

- [ ] **Lee, Nair & Weinberg 1992** — "Black Holes in Magnetic Monopoles" (Phys. Rev. D 45, 2751; hep-th/9112008)
  - Closest prior work on monopole-to-BH transitions. Must explain how our proposal differs.

## Tier 2: Read During Derivation Phase

- [ ] **Page 1976** — Particle emission rates from Schwarzschild BHs
  - Greybody factors. Our species counting must reproduce his results.

- [ ] **Hawking 1975** — "Particle Creation by Black Holes" (Commun. Math. Phys. 43, 199)
  - Foundation. T_H derivation.

- [ ] **Alford et al. 2001** — "Illuminating Dense Quark Matter" (hep-ph/0107211)
  - CFL electromagnetic properties: the rotated photon, reflection at CFL/vacuum interface.
  - **Critical for Section 4 (the mirror).** This is where we ground the CFL reflectivity claim.

- [ ] **Alford et al. 2008** — Color superconductivity review (Rev. Mod. Phys. 80, 1455; arXiv:0709.4635)
  - Comprehensive review of CFL/CCSC phases. Background for mirror material properties.

- [ ] **Lue & Weinberg 1999** — "Magnetic Monopoles Near the Black Hole Threshold" (hep-th/9905223)
  - Dynamic collapse. Relevant to formation section.

- [ ] **Gibbons & Manton 1986** — "Classical and Quantum Dynamics of BPS Monopoles" (Nucl. Phys. B274, 183)
  - BPS monopole interactions. Confirms exact force cancellation.

- [ ] **Bogomolny 1976; Prasad & Sommerfield 1975** — BPS bound
  - Short papers. Confirm the exact cancellation result.

- [ ] **MacGibbon & Webber 1990** — QCD hadronization of Hawking radiation
  - What happens to emitted quarks and gluons. Needed for mirror interaction analysis.

## How to Read These

For each paper, create a file in `notes/` with:
1. What they assume
2. What they derive
3. Key result (one sentence)
4. How it connects to our paper (which section, supporting or contrasting)
5. Any numbers we need to cite or reproduce
6. Specific quotes that are useful for our framing
