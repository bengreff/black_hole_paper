# Reading List

Prioritized by importance to the paper. Read in this order.

## Tier 1: Read Before Writing (these shape the paper's framing)

- [ ] **Crane & Westmoreland 2009** — "Are Black Hole Starships Possible?" (arXiv:0908.1803)
  - The paper we're extending. Understand their formation mechanism, mass choice, performance numbers.
  - Note what they assume vs. what they derive. Our paper must improve on their rigor.

- [ ] **Alvarez-Dominguez et al. 2024** — "No Black Holes from Light" (PRL 133, 041401; arXiv:2405.02389)
  - Kills gamma-ray kugelblitze. Our paper's motivation depends on this result.
  - Understand the Schwinger mechanism argument precisely so we can cite it correctly.

- [ ] **Lee, Nair & Weinberg 1992** — "Black Holes in Magnetic Monopoles" (Phys. Rev. D 45, 2751; hep-th/9112008)
  - Closest prior work. Shows monopole-to-BH transition at critical coupling.
  - Must understand what they did to explain how our proposal differs (engineering collection of many monopoles vs. theoretical single-monopole transitions).

- [ ] **Maldacena 2020** — "Comments on Magnetic Black Holes" (arXiv:2004.06084)
  - Must confirm our operating charge (0.027% extremal) is below the Landau-level enhancement threshold.
  - If Maldacena's enhancement kicks in, our Hawking spectrum changes.

## Tier 2: Read During Derivation Phase (needed for specific calculations)

- [ ] **Page 1976** — Particle emission rates from Schwarzschild BHs
  - Greybody factors. Our species counting must reproduce his results before extending them.
  - Power-rate coefficients: alpha_0, alpha_1/2, alpha_1, alpha_2.

- [ ] **Hawking 1975** — "Particle Creation by Black Holes" (Commun. Math. Phys. 43, 199)
  - Foundation. Read for the derivation of T_H = hbar c^3 / (8 pi G M k_B).

- [ ] **Lue & Weinberg 1999** — "Magnetic Monopoles Near the Black Hole Threshold" (hep-th/9905223)
  - Dynamic collapse. Does an incident matter pulse cause monopole -> BH? Directly relevant.

- [ ] **Gibbons & Manton 1986** — "Classical and Quantum Dynamics of BPS Monopoles" (Nucl. Phys. B274, 183)
  - BPS monopole interactions and the moduli space metric.
  - Confirms exact force cancellation between same-sign BPS monopoles.

- [ ] **Bogomolny 1976; Prasad & Sommerfield 1975** — BPS bound
  - Short papers. Confirm the exact cancellation result.
